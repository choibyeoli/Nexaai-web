## 📖 About Nexa AI
 
**Nexa AI** is a student-led, nonprofit education initiative founded in Okinawa, Japan, dedicated to delivering **real-world learning and business-level English education — entirely free — to students worldwide**.
 
We believe that access to quality education should never be limited by geography, economic background, or language. Through the power of AI-assisted learning, Nexa AI bridges the gap between local communities and global opportunity.
 
---
 
## 🎯 Mission
 
> *"To give young people hands-on professional experience, connect them with global mentors, and equip them with the confidence and skills to thrive in top universities and international careers."*
 
We envision a collaborative future where **AI handles the data** — diagnosing learning gaps, pacing lessons, and freeing up time — so that **human mentors can focus on what matters most**: igniting curiosity, building resilience, and guiding purpose.
 
---
 
## ✨ What We Do
 
- 🎓 **Free Education Programs** — Real-world learning and business-level English for students globally
- 🤝 **Global Mentorship Network** — Connecting youth with international mentors and industry leaders
- 🧠 **AI-Powered Learning** — Using AI to personalize and accelerate each student's learning journey
- 🌏 **Cross-Cultural Exchange** — Building bridges between local identity and global opportunity
- 📚 **Resource Development** — Creating accessible, high-quality educational tools and content

## 👥 Team
 
### Ren Otani — Founder & CEO

A high school student from Okinawa, Japan, and founder of the **Global Finance Library (GFL)** — a free, bilingual platform making financial literacy accessible to students worldwide. As COO of Nexa AI, Kai leads outreach, learning pathway development, and the **Japan Youth Investment Competition (JYIC)**, a nationwide initiative giving students hands-on investing experience.

## 💡 Our Philosophy
 
True educational reform isn't just about adding more technology — it's about **more empathy, more precision, and more time**.
 
We believe AI should serve as a tool that amplifies human connection, not replace it. By automating data-driven tasks, Nexa AI frees educators and mentors to focus on **building the next generation of globally-minded leaders**.
